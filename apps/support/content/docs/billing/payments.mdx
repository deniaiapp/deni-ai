You manage your plan in [Settings › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/billing).
Payments are processed by Stripe, and you pay by credit card.

- **Subscribe, change plans, cancel**: on the Settings › Billing page.
- **Invoices, card, billing details**: in the Stripe customer portal, opened with **Manage**.

Guests can't subscribe. Sign in first. See [Guest accounts](/en/account/guest-accounts).
For what each plan includes, see [Plans and usage](/en/billing/plans-and-usage).

## Prices and currency

Prices are on the plan cards in Settings › Billing.

- **From Japan**, prices are shown in yen. Elsewhere, they're shown in US dollars.
- You're charged in the currency shown on the card.
- **Yearly** plans show how much you save compared with paying monthly, and what it works out to per month.
- **Max Mode credits** are priced in US dollars. Checkout may show the amount in your local currency.

The currency follows where you're connecting from, not your account's language setting.

## Subscribe

1. In [Settings › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/billing), choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** on the Plus or Pro card. Go is monthly only.
2. Click **Subscribe**.
3. Enter your card and billing address. If you have a coupon code, enter it and click **Apply**.
4. Agree to the Terms of Service and click **Start subscription**.

Your card may ask you to confirm the payment (3D Secure).
The new plan starts as soon as the payment goes through, and the page shows your receipt.

If you've never subscribed before, plan cards sometimes show a limited-time discount ("% off").

## Change plans

Use this to upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and yearly.

1. On the card of the plan you want, click **Change plan**. For Plus and Pro, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly** first.
2. Check the **Estimated charge** in the dialog. The difference between plans is prorated.
3. Check "I have reviewed the estimated charge and agree to the plan change" and click **Confirm**.

The change takes effect right away, and the prorated amount is billed immediately.
If the payment doesn't go through, your plan stays as it was.

Your monthly usage pool switches to the new plan's size right away. Usage you've already spent this month still counts.

## Cancel

1. In [Settings › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/billing), click **Cancel subscription** on the Current Plan card.
2. Confirm with **Cancel subscription**.

Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. The Current Plan card shows "(Cancels *date*)".
After that date, your account goes back to the Free plan.

Until then, you can click **Resume** to keep your subscription.
While a cancellation is scheduled:

- You can't change plans. The plan cards show **Resume to change**.
- Max Mode is unavailable. Unused credits stay on your account until they expire.

<Callout title="Refunds">
  Access starts as soon as you pay, so paid plans are generally non-refundable, except where the law requires otherwise. Used Max Mode credits can't be refunded. See the [Terms of Service](https://deniai.app/legal/terms).
</Callout>

## Invoices, card, and billing details

Click **Manage** on the Current Plan card to open the Stripe customer portal. There you can:

- See and download past invoices.
- Update your payment card.
- Update your billing email, name, address, and tax ID.

Plan changes aren't available in the portal. Use **Change plan** in Settings › Billing.

## Team plans

Pro for Teams is billed separately, in [Settings › Team](https://deniai.app/settings/team). See [Pro for Teams](/en/teams/pro-for-teams).

## Troubleshooting

**"You already have an active subscription. Use Change plan or cancel first."**

You can only have one personal subscription. To move to another plan, use **Change plan** on that plan's card.

**The plan cards say "Resume to change"**

Your subscription is set to cancel. Click **Resume** on the Current Plan card, then change plans.

**A plan change failed**

The payment for the change didn't go through. Click **Manage**, update your card, and try again.

**Checkout doesn't load**

- "Failed to load Stripe.js": turn off ad blockers or other extensions that block Stripe, reload, and try again.
- "Checkout session expired": go back to [Settings › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/billing) and click **Subscribe** again.

**You can't delete your account**

Cancel your subscription first.

**Something is wrong with a charge**

[Contact us](https://deniai.app/contact).
