[Deni Run](https://run.deniai.app) is a web app where you give tasks to AI teammates.
Each teammate works on its own computer, with a browser, a file workspace, and a Linux terminal that belong only to your account.
It can research a topic, fill in forms, organize files, or run scripts without touching your own machine.

Deni Run is included with every Deni AI plan and has no billing of its own. It uses the same monthly usage as Deni AI chat and Flixa.

## Sign in

1. Open [run.deniai.app](https://run.deniai.app) and select **Continue with Deni AI**, **Continue with Google**, or **Continue with GitHub**. You can also select **Sign in with passkey**.
2. Confirm it's you a second time with a passkey, a code sent by email, or an authenticator app. Deni Run always asks for this second step.

Passkeys work only on the site where you registered them.
Email codes are limited to 5 per account every 15 minutes.

## Link your Deni AI account

Deni Run uses the plan of your Deni AI account. Until one is linked, teammates can't start any work.

If you signed in with **Continue with Deni AI**, your account is already linked. Otherwise:

1. Open **Account** in Deni Run.
2. Select **Connect Deni AI account** and sign in to Deni AI.

Once linked, **Account** shows **Manage plan and usage**, which opens [Settings › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/billing) in Deni AI.

<Callout title="Guest accounts can't use Deni Run">
  A Deni AI guest account can't be linked. Sign up for Deni AI first. See [Guest accounts](/en/account/guest-accounts).
</Callout>

## Give a teammate a task

Deni Run comes with teammates such as Chief, Research, and Operations. To create your own, select **New teammate**.
Each teammate keeps its own memory, skills, and routines.

1. Pick a teammate and describe the task, such as researching a topic, filling in a form, or organizing files.
2. Watch the teammate's screen live while it works.
3. Approve risky steps when asked. Results are saved in your workspace.

When a site needs you to sign in, enter a two-factor code, or solve a CAPTCHA, take control of the live browser, finish that step yourself, and hand it back.

### Approvals

Read-only actions run without asking.
Anything else, such as deleting files or running commands that change things, waits for your approval. To stop being asked about an action you trust, select **Always allow** on the approval, or turn it on for that tool in **Plugins**.
Every call to an MCP server you added yourself needs approval unless you've allowed that tool.

### Models

Choose a model in the picker:

| Model | Based on | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run | Claude Sonnet 5.5 | Faster, uses less of your usage |
| Run+ | Claude Opus 5.5 | Harder tasks |

Each model has four reasoning levels: Default, High, xhigh, and Max. Higher levels use more of your usage. The picker shows roughly how much each choice costs compared with Run at Default.

### Teammate limits

| Plan | Teammates |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Up to 5 |
| Go | Up to 10 |
| Plus | Up to 15 |
| Pro | Up to 25 |

When you reach the limit, Deni Run shows "Teammate limit reached". To add more teammates, upgrade your Deni AI plan.

### Files

You can attach up to 10 files to a message, each 10 MB or smaller. Attached files are saved to the teammate's workspace.

## Connect apps

In **Plugins**, you can connect apps so a teammate can search, read, and draft or send on your behalf.
Gmail, Google Drive, GitHub, Discord, Slack, Notion, Linear, Jira, Sentry, Stripe, and your own MCP servers are available.
Each one is off until you connect it.

1. Open **Plugins** and select **Browse connectors**.
2. Search for the app and select **Connect**.

When nobody is watching, background work can use only read-only tools from connected apps.

### Discord

You can also talk to your teammates in Discord direct messages.

1. In **Plugins**, find **Talk to teammates in Discord DMs** and select **Link**.
2. Send the code shown to the Deni Run bot in a Discord direct message. The code works once and expires after 10 minutes.

Discord server channels aren't supported. Steps that need the browser, such as signing in to a site, have to be finished in the Deni Run web app.

## Usage

Deni Run spends your Deni AI plan's monthly usage, shared with Deni AI chat and Flixa. It counts:

- Model use
- Time on the teammate's browser
- Time on the teammate's computer

Deni Run counts the cost after each step runs, so a task can go slightly past your remaining usage.

| | Free | Go | Plus | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly usage (shared) | 20M units | 60M units | 200M units | 400M units |

The pool resets on the 1st of every month at 00:00 UTC. In Deni Run, **Plan and usage** shows how much of this month's usage you've used, split by Deni AI Chat, Flixa, and Deni Run.
For exact numbers, see the **Usage** card in [Settings › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/billing).

When the pool runs out, Deni Run shows "Monthly usage used up", and teammates stop working until the reset.
Usage reset credits clear Deni AI chat and Flixa usage only, not Deni Run's. Max Mode credits don't extend Deni Run either.

To change your plan, select **Manage plan on Deni AI**. Plans are bought on Deni AI. See [Plans and usage](/en/billing/plans-and-usage).

## Your data

- Each account's workspace files, browser sessions, and computers are kept separate from everyone else's.
- Deni AI receives your Deni AI account ID and the cost of what you use.
- Messages are processed through OpenRouter and the model providers it routes to. They aren't used to train models.
- Deni Run doesn't use your data for ads or sell it.
- Tokens for connected apps are stored encrypted.

### Download or delete your data

In **Account**:

- **Download JSON** downloads your teammates, conversations, skills, routines, and usage. API keys are left out.
- **Delete account** deletes your Deni Run sign-in, workspace files, conversations, teammates, and billing records. Type `DELETE` to confirm.

Deleting your Deni Run account doesn't delete your Deni AI account. See [Your data and privacy](/en/account/data-and-privacy).
