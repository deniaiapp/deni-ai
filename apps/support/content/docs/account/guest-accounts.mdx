---
title: Guest accounts
description: What you can do without an account, the guest usage limit, and how to keep your guest chats when you sign up.
---

You can try Deni AI without an account. Select **Continue as Guest** on the [sign-in page](https://deniai.app/auth/sign-in), and you go straight to chat.
You may be asked to complete a quick security check first.

While you use a guest account, the account menu shows **Guest** and **Guest account** instead of a name and email address.

A guest account belongs to the browser you started it in. To keep what you made, sign up or sign in from that same browser.

<Callout title="Logging out deletes your guest chats">
  You can't sign back in to a guest account. When you log out, the guest account and its chats are deleted.
  To keep them, [sign up or sign in first](#keep-your-chats).
</Callout>

## What guests can do

- Chat with GPT-6 Luna.
- Use web search.
- Attach files, with up to 20 MB of attachment storage.

## Usage limit

Guests get **1,000,000 usage units** (1M). This allowance never resets.

- Chats use units based on the tokens they use, the same way as for signed-in accounts.
- Each web search uses 10,000 units.
- Check what's left under **Usage** in the account menu (shown as a percentage).

When the allowance runs out, sign up to keep chatting. Your account then uses its own plan's usage, not what's left of the guest allowance.
See [Plans and usage](/en/billing/plans-and-usage).

Single messages are also limited for guests:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Conversations this long are not available for guest sessions. …" | Start a new chat, or sign in. |
| "Files this large are not available for guest sessions. …" | Remove the attachment, or sign in to send it. |

For guests, the length of each answer is capped, and reasoning effort goes no higher than medium.

## What guests can't do

| Feature | What you see as a guest |
| --- | --- |
| Models other than GPT-6 Luna | "Only GPT-6 Luna is available for guest sessions. Log in to use more models." |
| Fast, Pro, and Deep Research | "Fast, Pro, and Deep Research are not available for guest sessions. Log in to use them." |
| Model comparison | Needs a signed-in account on Pro (or a Team plan). |
| Settings pages (`https://deniai.app/settings/...`), including plans and billing | You're sent back to chat. |
| API keys and Flixa | "Guest accounts cannot create API keys or use the Flixa extension." See [Use Flixa with external coding tools](/en/flixa/external-tools). |
| Signing in to other apps with Deni AI | "Guest accounts cannot sign in to other apps. Please sign in with a permanent account to continue." |
| Teams | Guests can't create teams. |
| Importing chats from other services | Not available. |
| Changing your email address, or adding a passkey, a Google or GitHub link, or two-factor authentication | Not available; sign up instead. The **Security** tab of account settings isn't shown to guests. |
| Saving memories automatically | Memories aren't saved from guest chats. |

## Keep your chats

Sign up or sign in **in the same browser** while you're using the guest account. Your guest data moves to that account.

1. Open the account menu and select **Sign up to keep your chats**. To use an account you already have, sign in from the [sign-in page](https://deniai.app/auth/sign-in) instead.
2. Sign up or sign in with any method: email and password, an email sign-in link, Google, GitHub, or a passkey.
   If you sign up with an email address, open the verification link in the same browser.

What moves to your account:

- Chats
- Model comparisons
- Projects and their files
- Attachments
- Memories
- Personalization settings, unless your account already has its own

What doesn't move: the guest's usage. Your account uses its own plan's allowance.

After the move, the guest account is deleted.

**Two-factor authentication:** if the account you sign in to has two-factor authentication and you sign in with your email and password, your guest data isn't moved. The guest account is deleted.

To sign up, see [Trouble signing in](/en/account/sign-in-trouble) for which email addresses you can use.

## If you don't sign up

A guest account and everything in it are deleted automatically:

- after you log out of it, or
- after about a week without use.

Before you log out, Deni AI asks you to confirm: "Log out of the guest account?"

For how we handle your data, see [Your data and privacy](/en/account/data-and-privacy).
