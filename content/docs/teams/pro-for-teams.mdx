Pro for Teams gives your whole team Pro-tier access with per-seat pricing.
You create the team, subscribe once, and invite your coworkers. Each member gets Pro access without a personal subscription.

Teams live in [Settings › Team](https://deniai.app/settings/team). You can also open it from your account menu in the sidebar (**Team**).

## What's included

- One usage pool for Deni AI chat, Flixa, and Deni Run
- Pro benefits for every team member
- Per-seat billing: you pay only for active members
- Centralized billing and member management, with an audit log
- Team projects shared with every member
- Team domains, so coworkers join with their work email

For what the Pro plan itself includes, see [Plans and usage](/en/billing/plans-and-usage).

### What needs a paid team plan

Creating a team, inviting members, and managing roles work without a subscription.
These features need an active team plan:

| Feature | If the plan ends |
| --- | --- |
| Team projects (creating or sharing a project with the team) | Projects already shared stay usable. |
| Audit log | Changes are still recorded and appear once the plan is active again. |
| Team domains | Domains you already added stop applying until the plan is active again. |

## Create a team

Guest accounts can't create teams. Sign in first.

1. Open [Settings › Team](https://deniai.app/settings/team).
2. Select **Create Team**.
3. Enter a **Team Name** and select **Create**.

The person who creates the team is its owner.
To create another team later, use the team switcher at the top of the team menu and select **New Team**.

## Subscribe

Only the team owner can subscribe.

1. Go to [Settings › Team › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/team/billing).
2. On the **Pro for Teams** card, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** with the switch in the top right. Yearly billing shows how much you save.
3. Select **Subscribe** and complete checkout.

The price is shown per seat. One seat is one team member.

For payment methods and invoices, see [Payments](/en/billing/payments).

## Invite members

Owners and admins can invite people.

1. Go to [Settings › Team › Members](https://deniai.app/settings/team/members) and select **Invite**.
2. Enter their **Email** and choose a **Role** (**Member** or **Admin**).
3. Select **Send Invitation**.

The person gets an email with a link. They can also accept from **Invitations for you** in [Settings › Team](https://deniai.app/settings/team).
Until then, the invitation appears under **Pending Invitations**, where you can cancel it.

When someone accepts, they join the team right away and your billed seat count may change.
A team can have up to 50 members.

<Callout title="Joining a team cancels a personal subscription">
  If the team has an active team plan, joining it cancels the member's personal subscription immediately.
  Unused time is credited toward future invoices, not refunded.
</Callout>

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Member | Use Pro access and team projects, and see the member list. |
| Admin | Everything a member can, plus invite and remove members, change roles, manage domains, view the audit log, and cancel or resume the subscription. |
| Owner | Everything an admin can, plus subscribe, switch between monthly and yearly, open **Manage** for billing details, and delete the team. |

To change someone's role, open the **⋯** menu next to them on the **Members** page and select **Make Admin** or **Make Member**.
The owner's role can't be changed here.

## Verify your email domain

**Requires a paid team plan. Owners and admins only.**

Verify a domain your organization owns, such as `example.com`.
Coworkers with an address on that domain can then join the team with their work email. They can also sign up even if the domain isn't normally allowed.

1. Go to [Settings › Team › Domains](https://deniai.app/settings/team/domains).
2. Under **Email domains**, enter the domain and select **Add domain**.
3. Add the TXT record shown at your DNS provider:

   | Field | Value |
   | --- | --- |
   | Name | `_deni-ai-verification.<your domain>` |
   | Value | `deni-ai-verification=...` (copy it from the page) |
4. Select **Verify**. When it works, the domain shows **Verified**.

If you see "The TXT record was not found yet", wait a while for the DNS change to appear, then try again.
You can remove the TXT record after the domain is verified.

### Choose how people join

Under **Joining by domain**, choose what happens when someone signs in with a verified domain email:

| Setting | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Join automatically** | They join the team as members. Each one adds a billed seat, and an active personal subscription is canceled. |
| **Suggest and require admin approval** (default) | They see the team and can select **Request to join**. An owner or admin approves or declines each request under **Join requests** on the **Members** page. |
| **Off** | Nobody joins through the domain. Its addresses can still sign up and be invited as usual. |

## Audit log

**Requires a paid team plan. Owners and admins only.**

[Settings › Team › Audit Log](https://deniai.app/settings/team/audit-log) shows a history of billing and policy changes made by your team's owners and admins.
It includes events such as members invited, joined, or removed, role changes, plan changes, and domains added or verified.

## Team billing

Owners and admins can open [Settings › Team › Billing](https://deniai.app/settings/team/billing).
It shows the current plan, the number of seats, and the renewal date.

- **Switch between monthly and yearly** (owner): pick the other interval on the **Pro for Teams** card and select **Switch**. A dialog shows the estimated charge. The prorated difference is charged to the team's payment method right away.
- **Manage** (owner): opens the team's Stripe billing portal.
- **Cancel subscription** (owner or admin): the plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then ends for every member. You can **Resume** it any time before then.

The **Billing** page also has **Team Max Mode** settings.

## Remove members or leave a team

Owners and admins can remove members. On the **Members** page, open the **⋯** menu next to the person and select **Remove**.
The owner can't be removed.

To leave a team, go to [Settings › Team](https://deniai.app/settings/team) and select **Leave team**. To join again, you need a new invitation.

The owner can't leave the team. Transfer ownership first: on the **Members** page, open the **⋯** menu next to another member and select **Transfer ownership**. They become the owner, and you become an admin.

### Delete the team

Only the owner can delete a team. Go to [Settings › Team](https://deniai.app/settings/team) and select **Delete team** under **Danger Zone**.
This removes every member and cancels the team subscription. It can't be undone.

If you own a team, you need to delete it or transfer ownership before you can delete your account. See [Your data and privacy](/en/account/data-and-privacy).
