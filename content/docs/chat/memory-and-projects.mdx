title: Memory and projects
description: How Deni AI remembers things about you, and how projects give a group of chats shared instructions.
---

Memory and projects both give the AI context you don't have to repeat.

- **Memory**: short facts about you, such as your name, language, or preferences. They're used in all your chats.
- **Projects**: a group of chats with shared instructions and a default model.

## Memory

### What gets saved

There are two kinds of memories:

| Kind | How it's added |
| --- | --- |
| AI added | After the AI replies, Deni AI looks at your recent messages in that chat and saves lasting facts about you. |
| You added | You type it yourself in Settings › Personalize. |

Automatic saving looks for things that stay true: your name, language, role, tools you use, and preferences you always want.
It's meant to skip one-off tasks, passing questions, greetings, and secrets such as passwords.
It saves at most 3 memories per reply, and skips anything already covered by a saved memory.

Saving runs in the background after the reply finishes. When something is saved, you'll see "Saved to memory" with a **View** button.

Each memory can be up to 240 characters. Guest accounts don't save memories.

### How memories are used

Your saved memories are added to every chat as background.
The AI uses them when they're relevant and doesn't mention them unless you ask.

### View, add, and delete memories

Open [Settings › Personalize](https://deniai.app/settings/memory). Your memories are under **Saved Memories**.

- **Add**: type in "Add something Deni should remember about you" and click **Add**.
- **Find**: use **Search memories**, or filter by **All**, **AI added**, or **You added**.
- **Delete one**: click the trash icon next to it.
- **Delete all**: click **Clear all memories**. This can't be undone.

You can't edit a memory. Delete it and add a new one instead.

### Turn off memory

Turn off **Auto-save memories** under **Response Style** on the same page. The change is saved right away.

<Callout title="Turning off auto-save keeps your saved memories">
  With **Auto-save memories** off, the AI stops adding new memories, but the ones already saved are still used in your chats.
  To stop the AI from using them, delete them or click **Clear all memories**.
</Callout>

### Response style and instructions

The same page has settings that apply to every chat:

- **Tone**, **Friendliness**, **Warmth**, and **Emoji**: how the AI writes. These save as soon as you pick one.
- **Instructions**: anything the AI should always follow, up to 4,000 characters. Click **Save** after editing.

## Projects

A project groups chats and gives them shared context. Each project has:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Up to 80 characters. |
| Description | Optional, up to 240 characters. |
| Custom instructions | Tell the AI how to behave in this project: goals, tone, constraints, output format. Up to 4,000 characters. |
| Default model | New chats in this project start with this model. You can still switch per chat. |

The project's name, description, and custom instructions are given to the AI in every chat in the project.
Your memories and personalization still apply too.

### Create a project

1. Open [Settings › Projects](https://deniai.app/settings/projects).
2. Click **New project**. The new project opens.
3. Fill in the settings and click **Save**.

### Start a chat in a project

- On the chat home screen, pick the project from the project menu under the message box (it shows **No project** by default). Then send your message.
- Or open the project and click **New chat**.

The project page lists your chats in that project.

### Archive and delete projects

- **Archive**: click **Archive** on the project page, or the archive icon in Settings › Projects. Archived projects move to **Archived**. You can't start new chats in them, and their instructions are no longer used.
- **Restore**: click **Restore** next to an archived project.
- **Delete**: archive the project first, then click the trash icon next to it under **Archived**. This can't be undone.

Deleting a project doesn't delete its chats. They stay in your chat list without a project.

## Plans

Memory and personal projects are available on every plan.

Sharing a project with a team needs a paid team plan.
On a team without one, the team shows as "(paid team plan)" and can't be picked.
See [Pro for Teams](/en/teams/pro-for-teams).

### Team projects

- If you're on a team, pick **Personal** or the team next to **New project** before you create it. You can change it later under **Team sharing** on the project page.
- Only the project's creator can change its team sharing, or make it **Only you** again.
- Team members get the project instructions and default model. Their chats stay private.
- Only team owners, admins, or the project's creator can edit a team project.
- If the team plan ends, projects already shared stay usable.
