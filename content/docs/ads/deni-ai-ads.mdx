## Ads in Deni AI

### Who sees ads

| | Ads |
| --- | --- |
| Free plan (including accounts with a verified card) | Shown |
| Guests | Shown |
| Visitors who aren't signed in | Only on the public home page |
| Go, Plus, Pro, and Pro for Teams | None |

### Where ads appear

- In a chat, below the latest response, once the response has finished
- On the new chat screen, below the message box
- On the public home page at [deniai.app](https://deniai.app)

Each ad is text only, with a title, a short description, and a **Learn more** button. It's labeled **Advertisement**.
**Learn more** opens the advertiser's site in a new tab. Deni AI doesn't send the page you came from to that site.

### How ads are chosen

Ads are picked at random from the approved ads for your display language, Japanese or English.
Deni AI doesn't use your conversations or your Google sign-in profile to choose ads.

Views and clicks are recorded with your account. For visitors without an account, they're recorded with a scrambled form of the IP address, not the address itself.
For more, see [Your data and privacy](/en/account/data-and-privacy).

### Remove ads

Upgrade to Go, Plus, or Pro. See [Plans and usage](/en/billing/plans-and-usage).

[Settings › Ads](https://deniai.app/settings/ads) doesn't turn ads off. It's where advertisers manage their ads.

### Report an ad

There's no button to report or hide an ad.
If an ad looks like a scam, impersonates someone, or is otherwise inappropriate, [contact us](/en/contact) with the ad's title and the site it links to.

## Advertise with Deni AI Ads

Deni AI Ads lets you show a text ad to Deni AI users. Your ad is reviewed by AI, and you pay in advance to publish it.

Anyone with a Deni AI account can advertise, on any plan. Guests can't.
Your own ads aren't shown to you.

### Prices

| Plan | Price | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Views (CPM) | ¥200 per 1,000 views | Prepaid, ¥300–¥100,000 |
| Clicks (CPC) | ¥30 per click | Prepaid, ¥300–¥100,000 |
| Fixed slot | ¥3,000 for 30 days | ¥3,000 |

Prices are in Japanese yen. Payment is by card through Stripe, and promotion codes are accepted.

A fixed slot reserves the ad space in chat for 30 days from when it starts. Only one fixed slot can run at a time across Deni AI. While one is running, it's the only ad shown.

### Create an ad

1. Open [Settings › Ads](https://deniai.app/settings/ads).
2. Fill in the form:

   | Field | Rules |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Default ad language** | Japanese or English |
   | **Ad title** | 3–100 characters |
   | **Ad description** | 10–240 characters |
   | **Japanese variant** or **English variant** | Optional. The same text in the other language, with the same limits |
   | **Destination URL (HTTPS)** | See [Destination URL](#destination-url) |
   | **Target languages** | Japanese, English, or both |

3. Choose a plan and enter your budget.
4. Select **Submit for AI review**. The review takes a moment.
5. If the ad is approved, select **Pay and publish** and complete the payment.

Viewers see the variant in their display language. If there isn't one, they see the default text.
Your ad is shown only to people using Deni AI in the target languages you chose. That's the only targeting option.

You can submit up to 10 new ads in 24 hours. After that, you'll see "Daily submission limit reached".

#### Destination URL

The link must:

- Start with `https://`
- Use a domain name, not an IP address
- Not include a username or password
- Be 2,048 characters or fewer

### Review

Every ad is reviewed by AI before you can pay. The review can't open your site, so it checks only the ad text and URL.

Ads are rejected for:

- Scams or phishing
- Impersonating a person or company
- Malware
- Illegal goods or services
- Sexual content
- Hate or violence
- Dangerous medical or financial claims

Ordinary marketing language and comparisons with other products are allowed. Mentioning a competitor by name isn't impersonation.

If your ad is rejected, the reason is shown on the campaign card. Select **Edit** to fix it and submit it again.
If you see "Unable to submit ad", the review couldn't run. Try again later.

Approval doesn't mean the ad is legal or will perform well. You're responsible for your ad's claims and your site.

### Campaign status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Under review | The AI review is running |
| Approved | Ready to pay |
| Awaiting payment | Checkout is open. Select **Resume payment** to finish it |
| Payment received, activation pending | Your payment is being confirmed. Don't pay again |
| Active | Being shown |
| Rejected | Didn't pass the review, or the fixed slot was taken |
| Paused | Stopped because the payment was refunded or disputed |
| Ended | The budget ran out or the 30 days ended |

The campaign list has **All**, **Active**, **Rejected**, and **Ended** filters.
Each campaign shows its billable views and clicks, and **Spent** against your budget.

If you see "Payment is being verified. Do not pay again." for a long time, [contact us](/en/contact).

### How views and clicks are billed

- A view counts when at least half of the ad is on screen.
- Views are billed in groups of five (¥1).
- Views and clicks from guests count as half.
- Repeat views by the same person within 10 minutes, and repeat clicks within the same day, aren't billed.
- Your ad stops when your prepaid budget is used up.

The view and click counts on the campaign card are billable counts, so guest views count as 0.5.

### Edit an ad

You can edit an ad that's **Active**, **Approved**, or **Rejected** (before you've paid).

- Changes to the title, description, or URL are reviewed by AI before they go live. If a change is rejected, the live ad stays as it was and you'll see "Ad edit rejected" with the reason.
- **Target languages** can be changed without a review.
- The plan and budget can't be changed.

You can request up to 10 edit reviews a day.

### Pause, stop, or refund

You can't pause or delete a campaign yourself. A campaign stops on its own when its budget runs out or its 30 days end.

If you pay for a fixed slot that's already taken, the campaign is rejected and your payment is refunded automatically. You'll see "Fixed slot unavailable; payment refunded".

For anything else, such as unused budget or a billing mistake, [contact us](/en/contact). Include the ad's title and the date you paid.
